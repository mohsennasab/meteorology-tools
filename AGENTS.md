# Project writing standard

Apply these rules to documentation, source comments, log messages, plot text,
and other user-facing language:

- Use direct, plain language.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes. Use a comma, colon, parentheses, or a
  standard hyphen when needed.
- Remove filler, sales language, vague claims, and formulaic phrases.
- Avoid unnecessary adverbs and adjectives.
- State the action, input, output, assumption, or limit without added emphasis.
- Keep technical terms when they improve accuracy. Define terms that may be
  unfamiliar to the intended reader.
